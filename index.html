About RESCO Organization

Renewable Energy System Co. LLC

Renewable Energy System Co. LLC (RESCO), legally registered in Oman, and Resco Technical & Trading Co. Ltd. in Kenya, provide products and services of the highest standards across industrial, hospitality, automotive, and project sectors.

The company holds an excellent reputation in Kenya and Oman for outstanding performance and a highly qualified technical team. RESCO combines the latest technology with professional engineers to deliver innovative solutions in boilers, water treatment, instrumentation, mechanical, and electrical supply and services.

Based on market requirements, we constantly expand our product range. RESCO is committed to incorporating the latest technological developments to provide effective, efficient solutions that generate real cost benefits.

  • Industrial Sectors
  • Hotel Industry
  • Automotive and Solar Batteries
  • Interior Designing and Projects

Vision

To establish RESCO as the leading local maintenance, trading, and turnkey service provider in Kenya & Oman by delivering high-quality services and products with customer satisfaction being the main aim.

Mission

RESCO aims to provide a one stop solution to strengthen sustainable value for all stakeholders through enhancing the effectiveness and efficiency of staff and operations to achieve customer satisfaction as well as public recognition.

Our Fit For Purpose Brand Promise

For RESCO, every energy transition must be adapted to each reality. Our fit for purpose approach reflects our commitment to developing customized solutions that exactly meet everyone's needs. By simplifying access to renewable energy and industrial systems, we are building a path towards a sustainable future.
